# The asset and feel pipeline

This pipeline owns how Hungry God Inc. looks, moves and sounds: the documents that define it and the code that generates it. It knows nothing about the rules of the game. The target layout and the door to the game are in `docs/architecture.md` §1.

## Documents this pipeline keeps current
- The style, as checkable rules: `docs/style.md` (moves to `feel/docs/` with the folder move).
- The theme (what things are: the people, their buildings, their world): `docs/theme.md`.
- The references: `docs/refs/mine.png` (line and light for the whole game), `docs/refs/town-target.png` (the town's detail; mushrooms bigger, darker).
- The town as a place: `docs/town.md`.

## How a look gets into the game
0. The bar for all art is the approved interface art (`screenshots/ui5-forge.png`, `ui5-apothecary.png`, `ui2-card.png`): an illustration with dozens of deliberate marks, drawn large and baked down. A few shapes with an outline is not art and is not accepted.
1. One builder draws ONE proof (a frame or a 1:1 crop beside the reference) and stops.
2. The owner judges it. Nothing is built further on a look the owner has not approved.
3. Approved static art (ground, buildings, props) is baked to image files in `assets/` and placed by the game from a small table; its generator stays here and is not loaded by the game.
4. What must be live (people, crystals, effects, weather, light, sound) is exposed to the game through `feel/runtime` only.
5. This table is updated in the same commit.
