## Build: `src/render/people/` — one people module for the mine now and the town later
1. **Figure design** like the reference: small lanky mine folk — pale round head, thin neck and limbs, slightly hunched, work clothes (tunic/apron, wraps, boots), headgear and tools by role (Digger: pick, lamp cap; Hauler: harness, pushes carts/carries crates; Sorter: apron, basket; Shrine-keeper: cowl, censer; Guard: helmet, baton, lantern; Overseer: long coat, ledger). Inked outlines, flat tones with one shadow step, readable at default zoom and good at zoom 3. Individual variation from the seed (height, build, posture, headgear, patches) so people are recognisable. **Scale:** match the reference — measure it (figure height vs corridor width and wall height in `mine.png`) and set the mine's figure height accordingly (about a third of today's); say the number in the handoff.
2. **A real rig and animation:** a small 2D skeleton (hips, spine, head, two arms with elbows, two legs with knees, hand-held prop) with posed keyframes and easing — walk cycle (with speed-matched stride, turning, carrying variants), pick swing with impact (spark, dust, chip — tiny), cart push, lift/carry/put down, sort, sweep, eat/drink, sit, sleep (lying, breathing), kneel and pray, talk in pairs (gestures, nods), warm hands, guard beat (walk, stop, look round), startle, shiver, glance, cower, collapse/death, crystallise pose. Blend between activities; never a frozen pose, never sliding feet.
3. **Behaviour (cosmetic, deterministic from seed + clock):** each person runs a routine inside their area: pick an activity spot that suits their role (use the dressing's work spots; add rest/social spots), walk there along the floor (simple waypoint paths that stay on floor and avoid props/rails where sensible), do the activity for a while, move on; sometimes pair up to talk; guards patrol. Spread people out; no two on the same spot. The sim's area is authoritative: when `zoneId` changes, the person walks the route through connecting passages (existing walking behaviour) with the walk cycle.
4. **Reactions and tasks:** `PersonView` gets optional additive fields (you may add them to `src/render/types.ts`): `task?: 'pray' | 'travel' | 'preach' | 'hide' | string`, `react?: { kind: 'whisper' | 'turned' | 'refused' | 'fear' | 'glance'; at: number }`. Followers show their task (kneel/pray toward the grave, walk, preach to neighbours who turn to listen, keep to the shadows) and a quiet tell (violet glint in the eye/lamp, a small secret gesture) — nothing over heads. Whisper target: head lifts, looks around; turned: slow kneel, then rises changed; refused: shakes it off; neighbours glance.
5. **Fog and light:** Sensed = dim gray figures still moving (cheaper animation rate is fine); Void = nothing; people take the local light tint if the renderer provides a sampler (request it; fall back to a flat tint).
6. **Crowds and town (design for, implement minimally now):** pose sprites cached per (design, pose frame, facing) so hundreds can be drawn; a `Crowd` helper (members loosely gathered, each animated at a low rate); town roles (Smelter, Trader, Farmer, Soldier, Clerk, Priest) and Elites as outfit sets — at least one outfit each so the town builder can adopt the module.
7. **Performance:** 30 animated people on screen ≤ 2 ms per frame of draw on this machine (sprite cache or cheap vector), no per-frame allocation churn.
