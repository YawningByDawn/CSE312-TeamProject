## Rules to implement (§15)
1. **Mining:** every unit with a rock-face job makes strike rolls on shift during the Day; a strike yields one crystal; rarity from one fixed luck table (six tiers, values 1/2/4/8/16/32) that only named effects can bend. Unconverted miners' crystals go to the cart → lift → the Tally. Followers pocket theirs (per-unit `stash`).
2. **The Tally:** the overseer's count at shift end; shortfall against the expected yield raises the Council's suspicion with a plain reason; searches at the lift can find stashes (crystals lost, follower in trouble).
3. **Routines:** each follower has exactly one standing routine — `pray` (default), `offer`, `lieLow` — changed by `setRoutine {unit, routine}` (1 action point). `move {unit, zone}` is a one-off influence attempt (1 AP, 2 if far) with a success chance that falls with distance from the unit's standard path (home ↔ work ↔ lift); on success their work area changes. Remove `send`, `recall`, intents, `task.kind` explore/spread/gather/goUp. Exploring gray ground and discoveries now come from followers living and digging there (and from Move).
4. **Action points:** `ap`/`apMax` (3, +1 per minor rank); Whisper costs 1.
5. **The night in order:** dusk offering (ordered `offered { unit, crystal }` events — rough crystals arrive in the player's inventory) → the draw (`miracleHand` of 3, `evilHand` of 3; `playMiracle {card, pay: crystalIds[], target?}` or skip; `playEvil {card, target?}` or skip; devour is an evil card; pools by Divinity) → actions → `endNight` → the Day (ordered events incl. `strike { unit, zone, rarity, kept }`, `carted`, `tally { expected, counted, short }`, `search`, plus the v2 ones).
6. **Followers' own evil:** each Day, chance by Malice that a follower commits an evil act unprompted: Malice falls by half of what the same act would give you; suspicion as normal; event with who and what.
7. **Crystals as items:** `Crystal { id, rarity, seed (drives the shape — the renderer draws it), rough, lines: StatLine[], effect?: OddEffectId, grafts }`; generation fully seeded; stat lines by rarity (1–4) from a pool (Nether a night, Hunger rate, whisper odds, strike luck, miracle power, suspicion, Ichor, action point on the rarest…); odd effects from Bright up — implement a long list (≥ 16) of strange ones, each with real rules, incl. Echo (summons a copy of a person, twice as likely to raise suspicion), Second Sight (one more blessing choice), Loaded (one reroll of a draw each night). Uses: `eat {crystal}` (Hunger down by value), burn (as miracle payment), `setCrystal {crystal, socket}` / `unsetCrystal` (sockets 3, +1 per Divinity rank; lines and effect apply while set), `cut {crystal}` → `cutChoices` (2–3 spreads; costs Nether) → `chooseCut {crystal, index}` (small chance to crack one tier down or reveal a flaw = odd effect), `graft {from, onto, line|effect}` (donor destroyed; graft cap by rarity).
8. **Blessings:** pick 1 of 3 at the start (the starting bonus) and at each minor rank (`chooseBlessing`); a buff or an interesting action.
9. **Feeding and pressure, watershed, town:** per §15.6–15.7 (lean town: crystal-handling groups and Elites, the vault with a guaranteed top-tier crystal, the gate ending).
