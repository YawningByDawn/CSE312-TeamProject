The animator makes a pose look right; **you decide what every person is doing at every moment so the place reads as a working mine and a living town.** Deterministic, headless, presentation-only (never changes the sim).

## Build `src/life/` — `createLife(layouts, seed)`
1. **Places:** derive named spots from the layouts — rock faces to dig, cart loading points, sorting tables, bunks (one per sleeper), benches, the fire, the shrine, the water, guard posts and patrol loops, the lift queue line and cage, and in the town: doorways, market stalls, the well, smelter yards, fields, the temple steps, the gate, street corners. Where the layout lacks spots, synthesise them on walkable floor (never on rails, props, pools, void) and list them as requests to the layout owners.
2. **Night (the player's turn, runs indefinitely):** `life.night(simState, t) → PersonView patch per unit` — night shift works in believable cycles (walk to the face, dig, fill a basket, carry it to the cart, rest, drink, talk to a mate, argue, laugh, warm hands), sleepers in their own bunks (turning over, one snoring, someone sitting awake), guards walking real patrol loops with stops, the overseer doing rounds with a ledger, shrine-keepers tending lamps; followers visibly live their task (praying toward the grave in ones and twos at odd moments, lying low near walls, a preacher gathering two listeners). Friends and rivals (stable pairs from the seed) seek or avoid each other. Small unscripted moments every 5–15 s somewhere on screen (dropped crate, a stumble, a shared drink, someone staring at the grave). Nobody ever stands frozen; nobody overlaps; nobody walks through props.
3. **Reactions that spread:** a whisper → the target stills, neighbours glance; a conversion → one neighbour stares, another hurries off; a miracle → people nearby flinch, kneel or flee by temperament; a guard nearby → followers hide their gestures; stained ground → the unconverted edge away or grow curious by temperament; a purge → scatter. Input: an event list with times; output: `react` patches and displaced routines.
4. **Day (6–10 s replay, driven by time 0–1):** `life.day(simStateBefore, dayEvents, t)` — bell → sleepers rise, the night shift downs tools; **columns walk to the lift, queue, ride up in cage-loads, spill into the pit head and walk home through the streets while the day shift comes down** (the lift never teleports anyone: capacity, queue, rides timed to `liftRide` events); sent followers walk their routes and do their task at the end (explorer with a raised lamp entering gray ground, preacher and listeners, gatherer filling and carrying back to the grave); enemies move as `enemyMoved` says; evening return. The same input always gives the same day; scrubbing `t` backwards works.
5. **Town life (from turn 1, even while black/gray):** market trade, carts on roads, smelter shifts, children running, priests' procession at set hours, soldiers drilling, drunks at night, doors opening and closing; density by district.
6. **Zoomed out:** the same schedules feed specks/crowd blobs (positions only) cheaply for hundreds of people.
