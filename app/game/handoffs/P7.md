# P7 — Opening cutscene: handoff

> **Round 3 start-up (orchestrator: "41 s of black after Begin"), 2026-11-23 09:30 — binding for callers:**
> the cutscene no longer waits for the mine. Its own art (memory, fall: ~1 s to build) starts at once from the
> spark; the mine's sliced bake (started by the title's `preloadMine()`, or by the cutscene's `enter`) keeps running
> between frames through the memory and the fall. Only the last shot needs it: if it is not done when the
> timeline reaches `HOLD` = 22.05 s (the shard sinking through dark rock, just before it lands in the grave), the
> timeline waits there on a **living frame** (the shard breathes, grit drifts; the score is held in step) and
> moves on the moment the bake reports done. Never black, never frozen; the bake is never forced synchronously
> (it can wait on a worker). A skip while the bake runs lands on that hold and completes by itself when the bake
> is done; with the mine ready it lands on the final frame as before. `?fast=1` waits for the bake. Harness:
> `__cut.mineReady()`, `__cut.holding()`.

## Interface (exact; changes additively only) — for FRONT (title + starting-bonus overlay) and PLAY
- **Scene:** `cutscene` (`src/scenes/cutscene/index.ts`, `registerScene('cutscene', …)`). Entered with any
  data object (e.g. `{ session }`); everything in it is passed through.
- **Hand-over:** on finish or skip it calls, exactly once,
  `sc.goto('bonus', payload, { transition: 'cut' })` with
  `payload: CutscenePayload = { ...enterData, world: CutsceneWorld }` and
  `CutsceneWorld = { renderer: MineRenderer, camera: Camera, view: WorldView, thread: { reach: 1, rest: 1 } }`.
- **The last frame** (t = 40 s; was 74 s before round 3) is exactly: black clear, then `renderer.render(ctx, camera)` with `view`, then
  `drawGodThread(ctx, camera, { time: view.time, reach: 1, rest: 1 })` (`src/render/mine/thread.ts`), all
  under the scene's logical 1600×900 transform. Nothing else (no veil, no dialogue, no UI).
- **Camera pose:** the very `Camera` instance returned by `whisperStartCamera(renderer)` (`src/app/worlds.ts`),
  sitting exactly on its targets (`x === tx`, `y === ty`, `scale === tscale`; at present x 1235, y 701.5,
  scale 1.3757). Do not re-fit, snap or clamp it on entry.
- **Renderer state:** the shared `getMineRenderer()` instance (seed `MINE_ART_SEED`). Fog: `grave` Active,
  `cartway` and `heartvein` Sensed (at the hand-over always replayed the same way: `settle()` in the shot's opening state, then the renderer's own fades in whole steps — never a `settle()` into the final state), all else Void;
  no people; every zone `control 0`, not selectable/highlighted/selected, no flags. Its composite is
  converged (`renderer.converged === true`). `view.time` = 117.4 (renderer clock = t − 22.6 + 100);
  `view.godPulse = 0.55 + 0.25·sin(0.8·time)`. Next scene: continue the clock from `view.time`, keep
  calling `renderer.update(view', dt)` + `render` + `drawGodThread` each frame; do not call `settle()`.
  First next frame drawn this way = the last cutscene frame, max pixel diff 0 (e2e).
- **`skip()` by phase** (Esc skips at once; Space/Enter/click/mouse-move first show a "Skip ESC" pill
  bottom-right, then Space/Enter or a click on it skips):
  - `prep` (two frames of the god's spark at FRONT's OPENING_SPARK 801.2, 356.4 while the cutscene's own
    art is built; the mine is not waited for — see the note at the top): the skip is remembered and runs
    as soon as prep ends (same as `play`).
  - `play`: dips to black over 0.35 s, jumps to 40 s in the black, fades in over 0.7 s onto the final
    frame, draws one frame fully clear, then hands over (identical frame to a full playthrough).
  - `skip` / `done`: ignored. `?fast=1` (sc.fast): resolves to the hand-over on the first ready frame.
- **Pause:** while `sc.paused()` is true (the shell's settings panel) nothing advances and the audio engine
  is suspended; it is resumed when the pause ends or the scene exits.
