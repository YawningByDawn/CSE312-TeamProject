### Done
- `scripts/sim-smoke.ts` is rewritten.
  - Bots: rush, cautious, devotion, evil, clean, reckless, neverEat, neverVent, massLieLow, random.
  - It prints one table per bot plus a summary. The columns are:
    - reach, loss, stall, p10/median/p90 turns, near-miss %;
    - per-band income, unspent Nether and crystals;
    - influence and direct spend as a % of income;
    - Hunger and Malice averages;
    - track peaks and threshold-crossing %;
    - Ichor lost by cause;
    - per-bonus win rate;
    - dead turns.
  - Usage: `npx tsx scripts/sim-smoke.ts [seeds] [bots=all|a,b] [maxTurns]`.
  - Trace mode: `TRACE=<seed> BOT=<name> npx tsx scripts/sim-smoke.ts 0`.
  - 40 seeds × 10 bots takes about 30 s.
- New modules:
  - `content/stages.ts`. StageDef holds the gate/end/defeat/dusk/level texts, hunters (home and texts), suspicion hints, cross texts, skim zones and cap, stationary roles, crackdown leader, cave-in zones, hideout and arrivals.
  - `content/affinity.ts`, the single source for miracle↔trait affinity. Night Terror is now fearful and grieving; bitter was removed.
- New rules. All numbers are in `balance.ts`, with doc comments.
  - Wariness adds +2.5 Resistance per follower beyond 2. Resistance is capped at 100.
  - Conversion odds use R/150.
  - Kiln vigils add 5 Resistance × cult level, max 3.
  - Order prices are `OrderDef.price × Direct × 1.5^(L-1)`.
    - Pray is priced along the Gain curve: `prayPriceK / gainRate(D)`.
    - Prices: scout 1, lieLow 1.5, smuggle 2, skim 3, preach 3, intimidate 2, shield 2, terrorize 3, sabotage 4, sacrifice 4, desecrate 6, frame 4.
  - Lie Low cooling halves for each further follower, capped per dawn: track 2, cap 4; public 1, cap 2.
  - Veins deplete. `zone.veinLeft` starts at 6 × richness. Bloom adds +3. A worked-out vein emits a `veinExhausted` event.
  - Gorge needs 5 crystals, then 3 more each time, with a 4-night cooldown.
  - Hunger rises by base 4, plus L, plus 1 per 4 follower heads.
  - Starving: Nether × 0.6, no hum, and every follower loses −1 Devotion per dawn. Ravenous resets Hunger to 70.
  - Hoard: crystals above 10 add +1 cult per 5 crystals per dawn.
  - Drift per dawn:
    - crests: +0.35 public per follower head;
    - quota: +0.25 government per follower head;
    - Kiln-lit crests: +1 cult per follower head;
    - public feeds cult and government continuously at public × 0.05.
  - Each miracle adds +1 cult and +1 public.
  - Suspicion decays by max(0.5, 2 − 0.5(L−1)).
  - A follower lost to enemies adds +3 Malice.
  - Outburst:
    - hits the zone with the most followers;
    - each person there dies with chance 0.4;
    - survivors lose −40 health and −15 Devotion;
    - all tracks gain +15;
    - pays evil XP;
    - Malice resets to 30.
  - Evil XP is the vented Malice / 2.
  - Follower XP is 7. Devotion marks pay 3/6/10/16 and a zone pays 12.
  - A lost zone costs −1 Ichor, and only after 2 slipping dawns (`zone.slipDawns`).
  - The crackdown re-arms once per stage (`flags.crackdownStage`), with chance 0.35 and a max of 4.
  - The mob hits 2 victims for 60 harm.
  - NEW `hostilePressure` in factions.ts. While a track is ≥75, it strikes with chance 0.6 per dawn and the strike kills, takes or burns with chance 0.5.
- Cleanup:
  - The dead exports are removed.
  - Texts take their numbers from BALANCE and the stage: eat, Lie Low, hints, Ledger with Kol alive and the Bunks, Intimidate "their eyes", the Fearful doubling in deltas, and curly apostrophes.
  - `spawnHand` uses `personalResist`.
  - `IllegalActionError` is raised for unknown or non-object actions.
  - The `bonus as never` cast is removed.
  - The `malice` fields are the single source.
- Narration is aware of headcount for whisper, convert and lapse.
