# Sim critic r2 (mine-only version, before the open-world merge): 6/10 — defect list for the sim builder

Engine praised: determinism, legality, exact spec formulas, property tests, clean structure, pacing (decent players reach the gate in median 19 turns), Hunger and "never evil" and mass Lie Low all punished, zero dead turns. Not mergeable because of balance and texts. The critic's bots and probes are in `/tmp/p1-critic-r2/work/` (`bots.ts`, `agg.ts`, `probe.ts`, `share.ts`, `hand.ts`, `consc.ts`, `ich.ts`) — reusable against the live sim.

## Major
1. **Nether piles up unspent in the second half.** Share of a night's Nether left at End Turn, turns 16+: cautious 56 %, decent 37 %, graveOnly 55 %, hoard 51 %, sloppy 65 %. Decent by bucket: ~10 % turns 1–15, 45 % turns 16–20, 54 % turns 21+. Cause: whisper cost does not scale with Divinity while income is ×2.25 at L3; each person can be whispered once a night and each follower takes one order, so sinks run out. (Seed 7, decent: turn 17 has 189 Nether vs 132 income; cheapest whisper 11.5.)
2. **"Typical action ≈ 15–30 % of a turn's Nether" is missed at both ends.** Median chosen whisper as share of that night's income (decent): turns 1–5 73 %, 6–10 40 %, 11–15 22 %, 16–20 12 %, 21+ 12 %. Orders cost 5–8 % late. On some seeds night 1 has no affordable whisper at all (seed 42: 8 Nether, cheapest whisper 8.6). `InfluenceCost` is never scaled by level (`selectors.ts:310`) while orders are (`orderLevelMult`, `orders.ts:150`).
3. **Careful play carries no risk; Ichor is never under pressure.** cautious and decent 600/600 wins; minimum Ichor stays ≥ the starting 8 in 268/300 (cautious) and 258/300 (decent) runs; near-misses 0–1 %; Hostile (75) reached in 2 % / 12 % of runs. shieldAll, stayHome, no-lift-push also win 100 %. "Mostly survives" should mean threatened but usually pulling through, with choices that matter.
4. **The cult (Temple) track is almost inert under competent play:** reaches Wary/Alarmed/Hostile in 48/3/0 % (cautious), 51/7/0 % (decent). Its hunter, rite and feast are essentially never seen. Government (Alarmed 85–99 %) and public (70–93 %) do bite.
5. **Starting bonuses are not comparable below expert play.** Sloppy bot, same 150 seeds each, win %: Cruel Delight 67, Quiet Rot 62, Old Scars 61, Deep Roots 44, Patient Hunger 44, Hoard of the Grave 38, First Dreamer 37, Lingering Throne 36, Sweet Voice 33 (a 2× spread; the repo's own smoke shows a similar spread with a different order). For decent bots every bonus is 99–100 %, so the choice is irrelevant there.
6. **Player-facing texts often omit or misstate the numbers the code uses** (23 sampled).
   - Wrong: Old Scars says "+4 maximum Ichor, and start full" but the god starts 12/14 (and `rules.test.ts:66` enshrines 12). Conscription "Let them march" says "−1 Ichor" but a Devotion ≥ 50 follower costs −2 and the event picks the strongest follower; hint omits public −3. Informer "Let them talk" omits public +2. Flooded-pools event "Send them down" says "(−1 Ichor)" but the drowning can cost −2; hint omits +2 crystals and +3 Malice.
   - No numbers at all: Pray (+8 base, ×1.5 Pious), Preach (5 + Int/20 Devotion, −4 Resistance, −2 cult), Intimidate (−8, Fearful −16, public +2), Terrorize (Resistance −10/−20, Malice −20, public +6), Sabotage (35 % kill, gov +8, public +3, Malice −25), Sacrifice (Malice −35, public +10, cult +4), Desecrate (cult +25, XP +25), Frame (gov −12), Dream Directive, Bless Their Bodies, Soothing Murmur (−8×power top track, −3×power others), Night Terror (−10×power), "Silence them" (vents 20 Malice).
   - Misleading: Lie Low says it hides from "inspections" (a follower lying low cannot be smuggling, so inspections never touch them); its "hottest Suspicion" means the hotter of cult and government only, never public.
7. **The stage goal is settled early, then it is an XP grind.** The lift is reached at median turn 9 (often via one Dream Directive); L4 comes at median turn 19; nights 10–19 are 11–12 rote clicks (pray/preach/lie low on every follower). The decision space collapses into "give every follower its standing order".

## Minor
8. Almost never used by purposeful bots: Vein Sight 0, Black Dew ~0, Sacrifice 0, Desecrate 0, Intimidate 0–0.1 per run. Crystal Bloom (cost 3, returns 4 crystals + bonus + XP) is an automatic cast.
9. Automatic opening: eat 5 crystals on turn 1 for a free Gorge (+1 Ichor while at 8/10).
10. `hoard` barely punished: holds 23.4 crystals late, still wins 97 %.
11. Odds divisor disagreement: `formulas.ts` comment says Resistance/150, `BALANCE.oddsResistDivisor` is 200, DECISIONS §4 says /100.
12. Order price deviates from the spec's `Direct = S^0.85·T` (charged `price × Direct × 1.5^(L−1)`); documented, but not what the spec states.
13. Dead/deprecated tunables: `BALANCE.publicFeedDivisor`, `quietRotMult` (@deprecated); orphan comment above `crackdownChance`; `flags.crackdownUsed` duplicates `crackdownStage`.
14. Stage-specific literals outside `content/stages.ts`: `'heartvein'` (Deep Roots), `'g01'`, `s.stage === 'whisper'` in `checkGate`, the ravenous stranger's reset to 80 and public +15, Seething's −15 % in the hint string; bonus effect strings hard-code 8 / 30 % / 10 % / 4 / 30 instead of reading `BONUS_NUMBERS` (only two of nine derive their numbers).
15. DECISIONS cites `handoffs/P1-smoke.txt` as evidence (fine in the live repo; keep it current).
16. Tests that miss bugs: Sacrifice test never checks crystal yield; Old Scars test asserts the buggy value; no test that event hints match their effects; bot tests in `round2.test.ts` assert only loose relations.

## Orchestrator's direction for fixing these in the open-world sim
- The open world is the natural cure for 1, 2 and 7: the town gives late Whisper somewhere to spend (more targets, farther reach, costlier and richer ones), so the back half is expansion, not waiting for XP. Scale Influence cost with what the target is worth (resistance, district, Standing), not with a hidden level multiplier that breaks the spec formula; make night 1 always afford one whisper (Ember floor); aim for the 15–30 % band across the whole run and measure it per 5-turn bucket.
- 3 and 4: pressure must reach careful players too — Temple attention should rise from things careful players do (preaching, converting the pious, being seen near the shrine/temple), and Ichor should be threatened a few times per run (target: decent bots dip to ≤ half Ichor in ~40–60 % of runs, lose 5–15 %).
- 5: re-tune bonuses so a mid-skill bot's win rate per bonus sits within ~12 points.
- 6: every order, miracle, evil act and event option states its real numbers, generated from the same constants the code uses; add a test that parses hints against applied effects.
- 8–10: give the unused actions a reason (or cut them); make Crystal Bloom a choice; make the turn-1 Gorge not free; make hoarding cost something (e.g. a hoard is seen/raided).
